## arXiv: From Emergence to Reversibility: Toward Reversible Multi-Agent Systems
- We propose \textbf{Reversible Multi-agent Systems} as a research agenda: collectives that grow while an authorized supervisor can still restrict specified effects, keep them restricted as agents adapt, and reconstitute useful cooperation under renewed authority.
- Multi-agent systems become capable through institutional mechanisms, such as rules that route information to agents that act on it.
- These mechanisms are also natural places to restrict the system.
- Yet as agents learn, share memory, delegate, and repair failures, the collective can find other paths to the same effects: a restriction on the original mechanism still operates but no longer stops them, while routine performance and compliance checks pass.

**Why it matters:** Tightens the multi-agent systems literature on a specific design axis.
