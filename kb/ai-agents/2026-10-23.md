## arXiv: Multimodal Embodied Agents: A Unified Capability-centric Perspective from Computer-Use to Robot-Use
- We introduce PAPAV, a unified capability-centric framework of five recurring functions: Perceive the current state, Anticipate action effects, Plan a feasible course, Act through an interface or body, and Verify the outcome.
- Multimodal agents (MMAs) sustain interaction through reasoning, memory, tools, and feedback, most visibly as computer-use agents, while robotic systems (RSs) couple sensing and actuation under physical dynamics.
- We define multimodal embodied agents (MMEAs) as goal-directed systems that couple multimodal task reasoning with physical action and revise decisions from the resulting feedback.
- This raises a central question: what changes when a multimodal agent moves from computer-use to robot-use?

## arXiv: Parallel AI Agent Collaboration as Graph-Structured Distributed Block Optimization
- Parallel AI agents can produce useful local edits yet degrade a shared solution when their edits are combined.
- We model this coordination problem as discrete block optimization with an exactly evaluable objective and a known dependency graph.
- Independent sets certify compatible edits whose verified gains, measured against the same current solution, add exactly.
- A coordinator constructs a safe compatible candidate and can also evaluate interacting edits to capture favorable interactions.
- We prove that an optimal fractional coloring yields a merger that preserves a $1/χ_f(G)$ fraction of the total verified local improvement, where $χ_f(G)$ is the graph's fractional chromatic number.

**Why it matters:** Evaluation methodology contribution — likely to influence future comparisons.

## arXiv: ToM2Vec: Continuous Theory-of-Mind Tokens for Multi-Agent Coordination
- To address this challenge, we introduce ToM2Vec, which learns to form abstract theories-of-mind in a rich latent space via a fixed budget of continuous reasoning tokens.
- We show that latent reasoning is critical, with performance dropping when reasoning is expressed in text.
- Moreover, we show that ToM2Vec training generalizes to partner model families not seen at train-time, including a proprietary model (GPT-5.4).
- Forming an accurate theory-of-mind (ToM) is key to successful multi-agent collaboration and requires reasoning under uncertainty to capture multiple possible partner states.
- This is hard to do in the text representation space native to large language model (LLM) agents, which more easily describe a single partner state.

## arXiv: OACap: Caption-Guided Active Observation for Detailed Video Captioning
- To better diagnose the information they preserve, we introduce Factual-Semantic Captioning Bench (FSCap-Bench).
- We propose Observation-Adaptive Video Captioner (OACap), a caption-centered agent that turns unsupported or missing information into targeted observation requests, uses spatiotemporal tools to acquire evidence, and iteratively verifies and revises the caption.
- Unlike question answering, where a query specifies a perceptual target, open-ended detailed captioning must determine what evidence to acquire and how to integrate it into a coherent global description.
- OACap captions consistently improve performance across general video-understanding benchmarks using a fixed text-only reader.
