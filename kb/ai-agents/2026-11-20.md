## arXiv: Asymmetric Decomposition for Multi-Agent Path Finding with Unassigned Agents
- This article proposes flow-oracle conflict-based search (FOCBS) for Multi-Agent Path Finding with unassigned agents (MAPF-UA).
- Robots without assigned tasks may obstruct active robots, while routing them to designated parking locations can cause unnecessary travel.
- FOCBS searches over assigned-agent paths using conflict-based search (CBS) and routes targetless unassigned agents through a min-cost flow oracle.
- When the selected assigned-agent paths leave unassigned agents no collision-free paths, flow-conflict branching converts min-cut blockers into assigned-agent constraints, which guide the search without excluding optimal solutions.
- A batched zero-cost warm start accelerates the oracle, and start-avoiding tie-breaking reduces unassigned-agent movement.
