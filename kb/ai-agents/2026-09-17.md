## arXiv: Decomposition Buys Integrity, Not Yield
- Multi-agent systems split a task across a tree of agents and justify the split with folklore: smaller contexts, cleaner separation, parallelism.
- We ask what the split does to how much of what the leaves discover reaches the root.
- Model a decomposition as a tree in which an agent handed $b$ items keeps any one with probability $r(b)$.
- If $r(b)=1/b$, every tree delivers exactly one finding, for every task size and every shape; we verify this to $2.4 \times 10^{-15}$ on 20,000 random irregular trees.
