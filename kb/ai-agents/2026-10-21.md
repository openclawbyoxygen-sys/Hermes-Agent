## arXiv: Cooperative Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning for Market Making on Prediction-Market Order Books
- We enrich the baseline market maker's observation space with the book and recent flow, redesign its actions and inventory controls, and propose a cooperative multi-agent decomposition into a quoter and a risk manager trained using recurrent Independent Proximal Policy Optimisation (IPPO) with RNN policies.
- Our redesigned single-agent market maker quotes more frequently and incurs smaller held-out losses than the retrained JaxMARL-HFT baseline, although the contributions of jointly changed observations, actions and risk constraints are not separately identified.
- A market maker provides liquidity by quoting prices to buy and sell an asset, earning the spread while managing inventory risk.

## arXiv: Topological Sheaves of Irreducibility: A Theoretical Framework for Multi-Agent Task Obstructions
- We introduce Topological Sheaves of Irreducibility (TSI), a sheaf-theoretic framework separating task-level compatibility obstructions from system capability.
- Multi-agent systems can differ under identical agents and communication budgets when they support different forms of joint interaction.
- A task is represented by an interaction complex $K$ and a capacity sheaf $\mathcal{F}$; nonzero $H^n(K;\mathcal{F})$ defines a structural signature, while registered interventions test capability.
- For cocycle data, cochain realization is solvable exactly when the represented cohomology class vanishes.
- Examples show that the signature is sheaf-relative, and the degree-1 reduction yields cycle-space witnesses and observation requirements.

**Why it matters:** Tightens the multi-agent systems literature on a specific design axis.

**Tags:** #multi-agent #evaluation

**Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26777

- Our study tested whether four AI trading agents followed their strategies and risk rules.
