## arXiv: UnitBoost: Managing Compound LLM Systems with a Merge Operator, Not a Model
- Compound LLM systems often solve a coordination problem by adding a higher-level LLM.
- The resulting meta-agent reads workers' outputs, writes the final answer, allocates later calls, and decides when to stop.
- It is expressive, but it also concentrates three control decisions in an opaque, order-sensitive model call.
- We ask whether the manager needs to be generative at all.
- UnitBoost replaces that model with a defined meta-level operator: a task-given unit map turns worker outputs into slot-value proposals, a constrained argmax assembles the output, and the slots left unfilled or unsupported become an explicit residual for the next round.

**Why it matters:** Orchestration / coordination of many agents.
