## arXiv: Self-Building Labs
- We introduce the self-building laboratory (SBL), which designs, configures, and operates its own laboratory setup given a high-level scientific goal.
- AI agents can increasingly propose new experiments, but existing SDLs can only run the procedures they were built for.

## arXiv: CocoRNA: Collective RNA Design with Cooperative Multi-agent Reinforcement Learning
- We propose CocoRNA, a cooperative multi-agent reinforcement learning framework for RNA inverse design.
- Designing RNA sequences that reliably fold into specific secondary structures is essential for understanding their biological functions but remains a challenging computational problem.
- CocoRNA simplifies the design task by decomposing it into smaller sub-problems, each solved collaboratively by multiple agents.
- This approach reduces the complexity of the problem and improves the exploration of design policies.
- During training, a centralized critic uses global structural information to guide the agents, enabling them to jointly optimize their design strategies.

**Why it matters:** Reinforcement-learning contribution within agent/decision-making.

## arXiv: On the possibilities and limitations of LLM-based multi-agent systems for multi-criteria decision-making in healthcare operations management
- We propose a framework for assessing the reliability of LLM-generated preferences as inputs to multi-objective optimization and quantifying how their uncertainty affects decisions.
- Multi-criteria models for healthcare operations need weights that express how stakeholders trade off patient waiting time, provider workload, and operating cost, but these weights are difficult to quantify.
- Teams of large language model (LLM) agents, each prompted to represent a stakeholder, have been used to supply them.
- We study explicit preferences by asking agents to rate the importance of the criteria in an outpatient appointment scheduling problem, and implicit preferences by inferring trade-offs from the agents' pairwise choices between emergency department configurations.

**Why it matters:** Reasoning-focused; relevant wherever chains-of-thought are studied.

## arXiv: Annotrace: A Workbench for Step-Level Annotation of Agent Traces
- The annotator writes their own labelling codebook; a frontier model proposes a starting set from a sample of their own traces, answers first, and is asked exactly the questions the human is asked, making annotation a joint human-AI process.

## arXiv RSS: Emergent Discrimination and Polarization in Multi-Agent Systems
- These results show how simple local learning dynamics can generate qualitatively distinct forms of polarization and discrimination, underscoring the need for population-level safety analysis of multi-agent systems.
- arXiv:2610.22701v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We study theoretically how discrimination and polarization can emerge in a population of learning agents.
- Each agent uses an adaptive linear decision layer to map a language-model embedding of an issue to a binary opinion and maintains an evolving estimate of how much it trusts every other agent.
