## arXiv: Strategic Tanking in Stable Matching Markets
- Motivated by a broad literature in psychology, organizational behavior, and matching markets, we introduce strategic tanking: an agent deliberately makes themselves less attractive to selected potential partners in order to improve their eventual assignment.
- Arbitrary tanking, however, can go substantially further: under proposer-deferred acceptance, tanking equilibria can lie outside the sincere stable set.
- We show that tanking has a striking connection to classical preference manipulation.
- Unlike traditional manipulation in stable matching, a tanker does not misreport whom they prefer; instead, they influence how others rank them.

## arXiv: Renegotiable Options Fail to Prevent Hold-Up Between Two Profit-Maximizing Frontier LLM Agents
- Do contract terms that protect investment between people also protect it between profit-maximizing language-model agents?
- In the hold-up problem, a seller can adapt a unit to one buyer at a cost before the price is set.
- Once that cost is sunk, the buyer can offer almost nothing and the seller will still accept, so a seller who foresees this does not adapt.
- One remedy is an option: the buyer may buy at a fixed price.
- In theory the option stops working if the buyer can let it lapse and offer its own price instead.
