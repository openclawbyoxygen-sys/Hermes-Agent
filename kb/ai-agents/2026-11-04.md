## arXiv: VIAS: Value-Isolating Activation Steering for Multi-Value Alignment and Compliance for Organisations
- We propose Value-Isolating Activation Steering (VIAS), which contrasts responses to the same scenario under two prompts, one listing all relevant values and one omitting a single target value.
- Organisations increasingly deploy large language model agents that must follow organisational values.
- We check whether activation steering, which adds vectors to a model's internal activations during generation without changing its weights, can improve compliance beyond prompting alone.
- Since most scenarios involve several values at once, we focus on multi-value steering.
- A simple way to build these vectors is to contrast responses that follow all values relevant to a scenario with responses that violate them.
