## arXiv: Multi-Agent Screening: What Agreement and Acceptance Cannot Tell You
- We show what each kind of evidence can establish about their errors, even with unlimited data.
- When AI agents screen items for a costly human assessor, as in agentic search, e-discovery and AI reviewing of papers, only what they pass gets judged.
- Agreement shows that agents concur, not that they are right, however many there are.
- Acceptance by the assessor gives precision but only a lower bound on recall.
- Knowing which agents found which relevant items gives an upper bound on recall if agents' finds are not negatively correlated, and caps what one more agent can add if agents are interchangeable.

**Why it matters:** Tightens the multi-agent systems literature on a specific design axis.

## arXiv: Steering Emergent Behavior in Multi-Agent LLM Systems via Information Networks

## arXiv: PA-SaR: Perception-Aware Learning, Control, and Coordination for Multi-Robot Search and Rescue

## arXiv: ODSA: Orchestrated Discovery and Self-Aggregation
- We present ODSA (Orchestrated Discovery and Self-Aggregation), an LLM-directed scaling framework in which an orchestrator LLM is given existing test-time scaling methods as actions --- discovery of a new independent solution, comparison of existing solutions to build a better one, and acceptance of a proposed answer, which triggers its verification --- and directs stateless workers to carry them out.
- Test-time scaling improves the accuracy of a language model's answers by spending more computation at inference time.
- Established methods do so either sequentially, revising a single solution over several passes, or in parallel, generating several independent solutions and selecting among them.

**Why it matters:** Orchestration / coordination of many agents.

## arXiv: LoMASA: A Local Multi-Agent Sales Assistant with Confidence-Gated Delegation for B2B Quoting
- We present LoMASA (Local Multi-Agent Sales Assistant), a training-free multi-agent system that runs entirely on one desktop computer.
- Frontline sales staff spend much of their day re-coordinating quotes after customers change quantities, ask for discounts or new payment terms, or costs move.
- A 35B mixture-of-experts model (System-1) makes each decision in a single forward pass and reads its confidence from the probabilities of the answer options.
