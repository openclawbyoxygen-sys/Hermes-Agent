## arXiv: Imitation Bootstrapped Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning
- We propose Imitation Bootstrapped Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (IBMARL), a framework that extends Imitation Bootstrapped Reinforcement Learning (IBRL) to cooperative teams and uses R2BC to train the imitation policies.
- Demonstrations are an effective way to accelerate single-agent reinforcement learning (RL), but extending this idea to multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) is non-trivial as it is difficult and often impossible for a single person to teleoperate multiple agents simultaneously.
- Round-Robin Behavior Cloning (R2BC) addresses this by letting a single demonstrator control one agent at a time while the others execute their current policies.
- However, the resulting imitation policies are limited by the quality of the demonstrations.

**Why it matters:** Reinforcement-learning contribution within agent/decision-making.

## arXiv: TradeGauntlet: Evaluating Compliance Controls for Autonomous Trading Agents
- These results show why zero alerts alone do not establish an agent's compliance.
- Autonomous trading agents need controls that prevent problematic orders without making legitimate tasks impractical.
- We study this aspect of agentic compliance with TradeGauntlet, a replayable limit-order-book simulator that separates detection, prevention, and execution cost.
- In 1,680 simulated sessions with Claude Haiku 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5, all conduct alerts were self-matches.
- Gates eliminated those alerts by rejecting self-crossing orders, while the aggregate-gate condition reduced Sonnet's two-sided fills by about 15%.

**Why it matters:** Simulation-driven study — informs social-scale agent modeling.

## arXiv: More Agents, More Samples, or More Thinking? Untangling Coordination, Ensembling, and Test-Time Compute in Multi-Agent LLMs
- Evaluations of multi-agent language models often compare communicating architectures against a single greedy pass.
- This practice confounds coordination with sample multiplicity and inference compute.
- We synthesize nine primary empirical evaluations comprising 516 unique multi-agent configurations organized along a resource vector R with six dimensions and a baseline ladder with four categories.
- Against baselines with a single pass (Category A), multi-agent accuracy differences are negative overall and near zero when omitting the largest contributing study.
- When evaluated against baselines matched on sample count (Category B) or resource budget (Category C), multi-agent systems average -0.042 in accuracy, and multilevel random effects modeling on the log odds scale remains negative across sensitivity checks.

**Why it matters:** RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) contribution.

## arXiv: Collective Tree Exploration with Predictions
- For the maximum error, we present a deterministic algorithm that explores any tree with $n$ nodes and depth $D$ in $O(\text{OPT}+E)$ rounds, where $\text{OPT} = Θ(n/k+D)$ is the offline benchmark, and we show that there is no deterministic algorithm that admits an $O(\text{OPT})+o(E)$ guarantee.
- For the multiplicative distortion, we present several algorithms that induce a competitive ratio in $\widetilde O(\min\{ \sqrtμ\log k, μ\log\log k+μ^{1.5} \})$, where the $\widetilde O(\cdot)$ may hide logarithmic factors in $μ$.
- We study collective tree exploration with subtree-size predictions.

## arXiv: Physical-Risk-Aware Decentralized Agents for Constrained Vehicle Communications
- We develop a physical-risk-aware spatio-temporal graph attention (PA-STGAT) framework for agentic V2X control.
- Decentralized V2X resource control is challenging because each vehicle has only local observations, while reliability depends on network-wide interactions.
- Each vehicle perceives its local interaction graph, retains short-term temporal context, and independently selects its PC5 resource and power.
- Network outcomes are used during centralized training to refine the shared policy.
- Transmitter-specific credits map reliability failures to individual vehicles.
